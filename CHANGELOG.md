# Changelog

All notable changes to LahLah Voice for Android. Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com).

## [1.0] — 2026-11-08 · versionCode 1

### Added
- Dictate in the app: tap the mic, speak naturally, tap again. Fillers, stutters and spoken
  corrections ("Tuesday, no wait, Wednesday") are handled for you.
- LahLah Voice keyboard: dictate into WhatsApp, Gmail, ChatGPT or any other app.
- Gemini Nano on supported phones works out what you meant and fixes misheard words. Other phones
  use the built-in rules.
- Writing styles: Clear and concise, Casual, Polished, Very casual and As spoken.
- About you: notes and a writing sample so dictation understands and sounds like you.
- Dictionary for names and terms, and dictation history on the phone.

### Play submission
- Corporate account (Tertiary Infotech Academy Pte Ltd), package `sg.com.tertiarycourses.lahlahvoice`.
- Production track, full rollout, 178 countries and regions. Sent for review 2026-11-08.
- Data safety: voice recordings (processed ephemerally, app functionality), diagnostics and device
  IDs (Google ML Kit, analytics). Nothing shared. Content rating: all "No". Target audience 18+.
